About this website

Water leak information, made easier to navigate

Sacramento Leak Detection is an independent information and service-enquiry website focused on water leak questions in Sacramento, California.

What this website does

It explains common leak symptoms, how diagnostic work may proceed, and what to ask a service provider. Visitors can submit an enquiry describing their property and water issue for review and potential referral to an independent provider.

We are not presenting this website as a licensed plumbing company. The website does not itself diagnose a property, employ a field crew or guarantee that a provider is available for each service type.

What a qualified provider should confirm

  • Whether they handle the specific plumbing or pool system
  • Current licensing and insurance where required
  • Inspection scope and any applicable charges
  • Available appointment times and service boundaries
  • Responsibility for repairs, access and restoration

How enquiries are handled

If you call about a leak, you may choose to share contact details and a description of the issue. Those details may be shared with an independent service provider if there is a suitable match. You decide whether to hire any provider and should verify their credentials and estimate directly.

For urgent water or gas hazards, use appropriate emergency services. The site’s form is not an emergency dispatch system.

Transparent referral process

Before you hire any contractor

We do not claim to have a licensed field crew, publish fictitious customer reviews, or guarantee response times. No partner credentials are represented as verified on this website before a named provider has been identified.

  1. Identify the work. Describe your location, the type of leak and any meter tests or symptoms.
  2. Review any proposed referral. Ask for the provider's legal business name, California license number if the work requires licensing, and exact service coverage.
  3. Confirm scope yourself. Discuss inspection methods, written findings, charges, restoration, permits and whether the same company handles repairs.
