Leak Detection Folsom, CA

In Folsom, a changing water bill or persistent soil moisture calls for the same initial checks as elsewhere: fixture isolation, meter observation and verifying responsibility with the water provider.

Start with your water provider

If a suspected leak runs under a driveway, paving or landscaping, ask how the provider proposes to locate the service line before any excavation or restoration work is quoted.

City of Folsom utilities

Before a specialist is contacted

  • Confirm the provider shown on the water bill
  • Check fixtures and any irrigation schedule
  • Document the location and duration of moisture
  • Ask which part of the water line is privately owned

Questions people ask

Should I dig to find a buried leak?+

No. Locate utilities and get professional guidance first; contact 811 before excavation.

Can a leak be under pavement?+

Yes, buried supply lines can cross paved areas. Detection aims to narrow the source before access decisions.
