Clear information for evaluating water leaks around Sacramento properties.

Why the source matters

This site cannot confirm emergency dispatch or 24-hour availability. If water is approaching electrical outlets, panels or appliances, do not enter the affected area. Contact appropriate emergency services or your utility when conditions are unsafe.

The right next step depends on what is leaking, where the line runs and whether another source can explain the symptoms.

Signs worth checking

  • A burst supply line or fast-moving water
  • A ceiling that is sagging under water
  • Water near electrical equipment
  • A major drop in pressure with visible flooding
  • An overflow that will not stop after a fixture is turned off

How an investigation may proceed

Methods are selected based on site conditions and the provider's qualifications—not every job requires every test.

01

Keep people safe

Avoid standing water near live electrical equipment; if the situation is hazardous, leave the area and call for emergency help.

02

Stop water when safe

Use the fixture shutoff or the home’s main water shutoff if you can reach it safely.

03

Document the damage

If safe, note when the leak started and take photographs for the property owner or insurer.

04

Arrange a qualified assessment

An appropriate plumber or restoration service can determine the source and whether immediate repairs or drying are required.

Questions people ask

Can I wait until the morning?+

An active flood should be addressed promptly. If the shutoff stops the problem and the area is safe, a qualified provider can advise on the next step.

Should I switch off the electricity?+

Do not touch electrical panels or switches if water could reach them. Contact emergency assistance or your utility when conditions are unsafe.

Can I request urgent help through this website?+

You can submit an enquiry, but the form is not monitored as an emergency-dispatch system. For active hazards, use emergency services or a confirmed 24-hour provider.
