What to check before requesting help

Water provider boundaries do not always follow city limits. Locate the name on your water bill, note the service address, and identify whether the issue appears indoors, under the slab, in a yard line or in a pool.

Include these details

  • Your city and ZIP code
  • Where the water or moisture appears
  • Whether the water meter shows unexpected use
  • Any current flooding or safety hazard
  • The most useful time to contact you

Tell us where the leak is

Include your ZIP code and the type of system affected.
