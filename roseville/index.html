Roseville / water leak resources

Leak Detection Roseville, CA

Roseville has its own water utility and local conservation programs. A property owner should check the service address and utility account before relying on any rebate or repair-credit information.

Call (916) 555-0142

Start with your water provider

Roseville offers a 2026–27 leak repair rebate for eligible City water customers. The program can cover 50% of approved parts and labor costs up to $750, subject to available funds. It requires a free Water Wise House Call to identify an active leak, followed by an approved application before eligible repair work begins. A private detection visit on its own does not establish eligibility.

Official Roseville 2026–27 rebate rules

Before a specialist is contacted

  • Confirm the provider shown on the water bill
  • Check fixtures and any irrigation schedule
  • Document the location and duration of moisture
  • Ask which part of the water line is privately owned

Program rules / july 2026–june 2027

Roseville leak repair rebate eligibility checklist

  1. Verify the address is served by City of Roseville water and the current program is still funded.
  2. Book the free City Water Wise House Call; an active eligible leak must be identified by City staff.
  3. Obtain the application and wait for approval before repair work begins.
  4. For eligible approved costs, reimbursement may be 50% up to $750. Labor is reimbursable only when performed by a licensed contractor.

Official source: City of Roseville Utility Rebates (July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027). This is a City of Roseville program, not a Sacramento City rebate.

Questions people ask

Can Roseville customers apply for a leak repair rebate?+

Some eligible customers may qualify under a City program. Eligibility, prior inspections and approval requirements apply. Verify current details with the City before work begins.

Can any Sacramento plumber handle a Roseville water line?+

Coverage and qualifications depend on the individual provider. Confirm service area and licensing before arranging a visit.
