The site is hosted on Netlify, whose form processing service receives submissions when enabled for the deployed site. Service providers receiving referrals may handle information under their own privacy practices.

This version of the site does not include advertising trackers or a third-party analytics script. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to deliver the site and prevent abuse.

Enquiry records may be retained as necessary to respond, administer referrals and satisfy legitimate recordkeeping requirements. To request access, correction or deletion, see our contact information and explain that you have a privacy or data request. We will review requests subject to applicable law and operational needs.
