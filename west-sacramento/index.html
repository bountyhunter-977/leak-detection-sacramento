Leak Detection West Sacramento, CA

West Sacramento is a separate municipality from Sacramento, so water account questions and local programs should be verified with the proper municipal utility.

Start with your water provider

If ground remains wet between a meter and a home, avoid assuming whether it is utility-owned or private. Ask the utility about the meter boundary and contact 811 before any digging.

City of West Sacramento

Before a specialist is contacted

  • Confirm the provider shown on the water bill
  • Check fixtures and any irrigation schedule
  • Document the location and duration of moisture
  • Ask which part of the water line is privately owned

Questions people ask

Is West Sacramento part of the City of Sacramento water program?+

Not automatically. Check the actual service provider and program eligibility for your address.

What can cause a yard to stay wet?+

Irrigation faults, drainage, rainfall and buried water leaks are among possible causes.
