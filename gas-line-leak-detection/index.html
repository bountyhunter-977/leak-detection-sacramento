Gas Leak Safety in Sacramento

If you smell gas, hear hissing or otherwise suspect a natural gas leak, leave the area immediately. Do not use switches, appliances or a phone until you are a safe distance away.

Leave first. Call from a safe location.

Call 911, then contact PG&E at 1-800-743-5000. Do not return until authorities say it is safe.

Read official PG&E gas safety guidance

Signs that require immediate action

  • A sulfur or rotten-egg odor, even if faint or intermittent
  • Hissing, whistling or roaring near a line or appliance
  • Bubbling water, blowing dirt or unexplained dead vegetation near a gas line

This website does not offer gas-emergency dispatch or routine gas-line appointments. A qualified gas utility or licensed professional should manage any follow-up after the area is safe.
